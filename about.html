About the network

Alumni used to stay in touch through scattered social media groups. This website gives the institute one central, free place for alumni news, events and contacts.

Our goals

  • Help students reach mentors from their branch.
  • Keep alumni informed about institute news.
  • Support SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 17 (Partnerships for the Goals).

Who it serves

Current students, alumni, faculty and the placement or alumni cell coordinators.
